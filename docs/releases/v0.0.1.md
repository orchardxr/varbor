First public build of MacVR: Windows PC VR games on an Apple Silicon Mac, streamed to a Quest headset.

**How it works:** Windows game (D3D11 or Vulkan) under NotProton/CrossOver → OpenComposite (OpenVR → OpenXR) → our `wineopenxr` bridge → our WiVRn/Monado server on macOS → VideoToolbox HEVC → the stock WiVRn app on the Quest.

## Status

An early preview.
- **Tested:** Half-Life: Alyx on an M4 Max with a Quest 3S, at 90 Hz and about 89 fps, on both of NotProton's CrossOver 27 runners (FEX and Rosetta) and on the CrossOver 26.3 release.
- **Not tested yet:** any other game.
- **Supported:** D3D11 (DXMT preferred, or DXVK) and Vulkan games using OpenVR or OpenXR.
- **Not supported:** D3D12, OpenGL, Oculus-only titles.

## Requirements

- An Apple Silicon Mac on macOS 14 or later.
- Steam with NotProton and CrossOver (27 Preview or 26.3).
- Homebrew runtime libraries from the repo's `Brewfile` (`brew bundle`) and the Vulkan loader in `/usr/local` (LunarG Vulkan SDK).
- A Quest headset with the stock WiVRn app (26.9) on the same network.

## Install

1. Download `MacVR-0.0.1.zip`, unzip it and move `MacVR.app` to `/Applications`.
2. The app is ad-hoc signed and not notarized. On first launch, right-click it and choose **Open**, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/MacVR.app`.
3. Open MacVR. It sets up NotProton's runners and prefixes and starts the server.
4. Connect from the WiVRn app on the Quest, then start a VR game from Steam or from the headset's app list.
