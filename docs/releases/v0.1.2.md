## VarboR 0.1.2

- **Menu over full-screen apps**: VarboR is now a menu-bar app (no Dock icon), so its menu opens over a game or any
  app in full screen. On macOS 26 and later only menu-bar apps get that.
- **Windows and dialogs open on the desktop**: About, Check for Updates and the VarboR window take you to the desktop
  and show there, as sheets on the VarboR window. Before, a dialog could open out of sight and block the menu.
